# Product

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
AI Extract is a desktop OCR tool for Vietnamese administrative and legal PDF documents. It converts scanned pages to machine-readable text, then extracts structured metadata fields (author, document type, date, summary, classification, signatory, copy type). Runs 100% locally — no cloud, no network calls after setup.

## Users
Government clerks and archivists processing physical documents. Power users working with stacks of 10–200 pages per session. Occasional one-shot users scanning a single certificate. All users are Vietnamese speakers. They sit at a desk, often with poor ambient lighting, on Windows machines with 24–27-inch monitors. Processing is background work — they start a job, then do something else while it runs.

## Brand Tone
Precise. Calm under load. Trustworthy — this is government paperwork, not a consumer app. No playfulness. No marketing language. Dense information is normal and expected.

## Anti-References
- Notion-style whitespace product UIs (too airy, information-sparse)
- SaaS dashboards with hero metrics and gradient cards
- Terminal/hacker aesthetics (neon, scan lines, glitch)
- Consumer chat apps (too casual)
- Windows XP-era utilitarian gray (too crude)

## Strategic Principles
- Feedback during processing is more important than aesthetics at rest
- The output panel is primary real estate — not the sidebar
- Status must be readable at a glance from 1 meter
- Every pixel of chrome that isn't information is waste
